Readlet turns hard reading into easy reading. Open a paper, a book or a web article and read it bilingually with the original layout intact — every paragraph followed by its translation. Then let AI explain each page to you, out loud, while you follow along.

BILINGUAL READING THAT KEEPS THE LAYOUT
• Each page is drawn from the original vector PDF: formulas, figures, fonts and columns stay exactly as they are.
• The translation appears right below each paragraph; formulas, code, tables and references are skipped.
• Pages are translated ahead of you as you read; switch between Original and Bilingual instantly (⌘1 / ⌘2).

AI THAT EXPLAINS EVERY PAGE
• AI Narration explains each page in plain speech — including figures and tables — and turns pages for you.
• Five styles: Podcast, Storyteller, Technical Deep Dive, Popular Science, Quick Summary — plus your own instructions.
• Hold the mic to interrupt with a question; the answer is spoken and narration continues.
• Prepare All writes and voices a whole book in the background, with an estimate of time and cost first.
• See how much listening time is left at your current speed; start narrating from any page.

STUDY LIKE IN MARGINNOTE
• Excerpt with eight colours, add comments and titles, capture formulas and figures as image cards.
• Every card lands in a mind map you can reorganize; link cards across documents.
• AI Assistant summarizes pages or the whole document and answers questions with clickable page references.
• AI flashcards and AI mind maps help you check what you learned.

IMPORT ANYTHING
• PDFs, EPUB, Markdown, Word, RTF, HTML and text files.
• Web articles: paste a link and Readlet keeps only the article — no ads, sidebars or cookie banners — as a clean PDF.

YOUR AI, YOUR CHOICE
• Bring your own model: Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Qwen or any compatible gateway, or a local model with Ollama or LM Studio.
• Have ChatGPT Plus or Pro? Sign in with ChatGPT and use your plan — no API key needed.
• Voices: the free macOS system voice, or online voices from OpenAI, Google Gemini, Qwen-TTS, Azure, ElevenLabs, MiniMax and Volcengine.
• Keys stay in your Keychain. No account, no tracking: content goes only to the services you configure.

EXPORT
• Bilingual PDF, mind map and excerpts as Markdown (Obsidian-friendly), flashcards to Anki, narration as MP4 video.

Readlet requires macOS 14 or later. AI features need access to an AI model (your API key, gateway, ChatGPT plan or a local model); reading, notes and system-voice narration work without one.
