隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 10 月 16 日 · 适用于 Readlet for Mac（Mac App Store 版与直接下载版）

简单说：Readlet 没有账号、没有统计分析、没有广告、没有追踪。开发者不会收到你的任何文档或数据。

留在你 Mac 上的

文档、译文、笔记、脑图、闪卡、讲稿与语音、设置和用量统计都只保存在你的 Mac 上（Readlet 的数据目录）。API Key 保存在系统钥匙串。删除应用的数据目录即可清除全部内容。

会发送什么、发给谁

只在你使用相应功能时，并且只发给你自己配置的服务：

  • AI 功能（双语翻译、AI 解读、AI 助手、闪卡、AI 脑图）会把相关页面的文字——AI 解读和部分提问还包括页面截图——发送给你在设置中配置的 AI 服务（你的网关、API 账号或本机模型）。首次使用前 Readlet 会征求你的同意。
  • 在线语音 会把讲稿文字发送给你选择的语音服务（如 OpenAI、Google、阿里云、Microsoft Azure、ElevenLabs、MiniMax、火山引擎）。默认的 macOS 系统语音完全在本机运行。
  • 从网页导入 会像浏览器一样打开你输入的网址。
  • 语音提问 在你按住麦克风时使用苹果的语音识别。
  • 直接下载版 每天最多一次访问 readit.novarock.io 检查新版本（不发送个人信息）。

这些服务按各自的隐私政策和你与它们的约定处理数据。Readlet 不会附加任何标识，也不会把数据发往其他地方。

儿童

Readlet 不面向儿童，也不会有意收集任何数据。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026 · applies to Readlet for Mac (Mac App Store and direct-download editions)

Short version: Readlet has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We (the developer) never receive your documents or your data.

What stays on your Mac

Your documents, translations, notes, mind maps, flashcards, narration scripts and audio, settings and usage statistics are stored only on your Mac, in Readlet's data folder. API keys are stored in the macOS Keychain. Deleting the app's data folder removes everything.

What is sent, and to whom

Only when you use a feature that needs it, and only to the services you configure:

  • AI features (bilingual translation, AI Narration, AI Assistant, flashcards, AI mind maps) send the text of the relevant pages — and, for AI Narration and some questions, an image of the page — to the AI service you set up in Settings (your own gateway or API account, or a model running on your Mac). Readlet asks for your consent before the first request.
  • Online voices send the narration text to the speech service you choose (for example OpenAI, Google, Alibaba Cloud, Microsoft Azure, ElevenLabs, MiniMax or Volcengine). The default macOS system voice runs entirely on your Mac.
  • Import from Web loads the web page you enter, like a browser would.
  • Voice questions use Apple's speech recognition while you hold the microphone button.
  • The direct-download edition checks readit.novarock.io for a new version at most once a day (no personal data is sent).

These services process data under their own privacy policies and your agreement with them. Readlet does not add any identifiers or send data anywhere else.

Children

Readlet is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data.
